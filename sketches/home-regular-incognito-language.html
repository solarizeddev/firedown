Flat rows. Chips dimmed → FAB is the only saturated coral. One hero.

What carries over: flat rows (ripple-tappable, like a settings list), small chips as colour landmarks, a single hero FAB. The chips keep their hue identity (coral/raspberry/peach) but at tonal-container chroma — dim enough that the Downloads chip no longer twins the coral FAB, so the FAB is unmistakably the one accent.

Honest caveat: regular can't borrow incognito's tinted canvas — it's the neutral mode, a purple regular home would be wrong. So this gets incognito's calm and balance, but not the coral-on-purple punch. A small optional touch to recover some warmth: a faint "Home" header (shown) gives the screen the same titled-anchor incognito has.

This replaces the Tonal/Neutral/default debate: if regular adopts this, the heavy filled-card "Tonal" and the flat "Neutral" both fold into ONE balanced default, and the styles picker could even retire (or keep only Coral as the "I want the loud version" opt-in).
