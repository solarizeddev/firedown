‹ How sync works
Is everything encrypted?

Yes — bookmarks and backed-up files are encrypted on this device before they leave it. The server only ever stores scrambled data it can't read.

What is the recovery code?

One key, generated on your device, that unlocks both your bookmarks and your file backups. It's the only thing that can decrypt them.

What if I lose it?

Your synced data can't be recovered — not by you, not by us. There's no password reset, because we never had the key.

Do I need an account or email?

No. Just the recovery code. Even paid storage stays anonymous — payment isn't linked to your files.

What does "pay as you go" mean?

Bookmarks are free. Backing up files uses real storage, so you buy anonymous credits for what you use. Stop any time.
