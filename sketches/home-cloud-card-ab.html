Why ghost wins per style — Coral: the one non-filled card instantly reads "not active yet", intentional rather than foreign (the failure mode of a blue full card). Tonal: ghost vs three brand chips = "available, not enabled". Neutral: already greyscale, fits free. And the payoff is product, not just visual: a quiet ghost card is an invitation; a loud always-on cloud card is nagware. On connect it adopts the brand coral as its "active/premium" state — colour becomes a reward.
My pick: Option B. It solves the 4th-colour problem in all three styles at once, it's honest about Cloud being an upsell (off for most users), and it gives you a built-in "win" moment when they connect. Option A is the safe fallback if you'd rather Cloud always look like a first-class peer from day one — at the cost of a slightly monotonous warm wall. Either way: nothing to build now — Cloud isn't implemented. This just locks the direction.
