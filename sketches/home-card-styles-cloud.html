Cloud's accent is deliberately COOL (sky #6EA8E8), not another warm brand tone. The other three cards are local device features (downloads, vault, on-device blocking) — all warm. Making Cloud the one cool card signals "this one lives somewhere else / it's the sync+monetize tier" at a glance, without a label. Trade-off you should weigh:
  • Tonal (default): the cool chip on the grey card reads great — clear odd-one-out, no clash. This is the strongest argument for the cool choice.
  • Coral: a full blue card among three warm ones is the riskiest — it can look foreign rather than intentional. If that bothers you, Cloud in Coral could instead take a warm gold (#E7C06A) so the wall-of-warmth stays coherent and Cloud is just the "premium" gold.
  • Neutral: greyscale anyway, so Cloud is just a 4th grey card — Cloud's identity comes only from the ☁ glyph. Fine, and on-brand for the "calm" pick.
